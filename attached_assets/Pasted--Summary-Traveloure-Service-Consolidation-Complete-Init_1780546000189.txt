# Summary: Traveloure Service Consolidation (Complete)

## Initial Problem
You requested consolidation of expert and provider service management. The codebase had services spread across three tables (provider_services, expert_custom_services, expert_service_offerings) with divergent workflows and duplicate code in the UI.

## Architectural Decision
After audit, you discovered the immutable structural constraint: `service_bookings.serviceId` and `service_reviews.serviceId` both FK to `provider_services.id`. This means:
- **provider_services is the canonical source** — no alternative is viable without fragmenting the booking/review/payment path
- expert_custom_services and expert_service_offerings become read-only reference catalogs
- User role distinction happens via read-time filtering (expert vs provider), not separate tables
- Category mapping is critical: services migrated without mapping become invisible to feeds/browse/matching
