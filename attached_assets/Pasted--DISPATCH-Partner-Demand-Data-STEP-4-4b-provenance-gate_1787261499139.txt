The question: are the 27 trips real independent demand converging on a date, or a creation-batch artifact that survives R16 because its owners aren't `@traveloure.test`?

**A1 — The distribution query.** For the trips behind the Sep 13 stay cell (market kyoto, no stay anchored, check-in 2026-09-13, R16-passing):
```sql
SELECT t.id, t.created_at::date AS created, u.email,
       t.start_date, t.end_date, t.origin_market,
       (t.end_date - t.start_date) AS len_days
FROM trips t LEFT JOIN users u ON u.id = t.user_id
WHERE lower(t.destination) ~ 'kyoto' AND t.start_date = '2026-09-13'
ORDER BY t.created_at;
-- plus the aggregate shape:
SELECT t.created_at::date AS created_day, count(*),
       count(DISTINCT t.user_id) AS distinct_users,
       count(DISTINCT (t.end_date - t.start_date)) AS distinct_lengths
FROM trips t WHERE lower(t.destination) ~ 'kyoto' AND t.start_date = '2026-09-13'
GROUP BY 1 ORDER BY 1;
```
