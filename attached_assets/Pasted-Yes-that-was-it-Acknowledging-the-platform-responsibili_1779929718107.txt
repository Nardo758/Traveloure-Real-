Choose which account type fits your use case:

Express (recommended for most platforms):

Stripe handles onboarding and verification
Connected accounts get a light Express Dashboard
You control payout settings and charge types
Best for marketplaces where you manage most of the experience
Standard:

Connected accounts get full Stripe Dashboard access
They have a direct relationship with Stripe
Less control for you, more independence for them
Best when your experts/providers are already familiar with Stripe
Custom:

You control everything, connected accounts never see Stripe
Most complex integration
You build the dashboard and collect all verification info
