So 7 people means **7 seats** for a P1 scheduled place service, **ONE booking with a guest count of 7** for a P6 stay, and **per-component counts** for a P7 bundle — and `quantity: 7` bills the villa seven times. The commerce contract already treats them as different facts (P1's `requiredContext` names `party_size`, P6's names `guests`; neither is `quantity`), and `provider_services` carries `partySizeMin`/`partySizeMax` with nowhere on the cart row to hold the traveler's answer. Recorded as **punchlist D-14**, blocking **OC-B3**; the recommendation is that `quantity` keeps meaning units of the listing and the party becomes its own required-context fact, because collapsing two answers into one number is the defect. Capacity is claimed at checkout and never at add-to-cart, so this is a question about what gets BILLED, not a race. | `docs/PUNCHLIST.md` |
