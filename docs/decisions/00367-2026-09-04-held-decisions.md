| 2026-09-04-held-decisions | 2026-09-04 | [advisory] | **FOUR THINGS DELIBERATELY NOT BUILT, each recorded so the absence is a decision rather than an oversight.** **(1) ROOM BLOCKS — HELD until a provider asks for them.** A room block is a listing-side QUANTITY concept (n rooms held against a date range under one rate), not a plan-side one, and `provider_services` already carries a concurrency field — **`maxConcurrentBookings`** — which is where any quantity answer would have to start. Building the traveler-facing half before a single provider has asked to sell that way would ship a surface with nothing behind it. **(2) SITE SEARCH — HELD until more than one market is stocked.** A search box over one stocked market returns that market or nothing; it teaches a traveler that the platform is empty (§13), and it is not the honest surface for a supply problem. Revisit when the second market is stocked. **(3) THE EXECUTIVE ASSISTANT GETS NO PUBLIC STOREFRONT.** The EA is a B2B role with its own `/ea/*` console, not a listing anyone browses and books. `client/src/lib/earn-roles.ts` already says so — `EA_SIGNUP` is the deferred fifth card, "real signup, no offering backing yet" — and this ruling makes that permanent rather than a gap waiting to be filled: an EA is hired through a relationship, and a storefront would imply a catalog, a price and a booking rail that do not exist. **(4) GUEST TRIPS (G2) STAY HELD as a separate architecture.** A trip-less visitor's cart is the sanctioned fallback today (CLAUDE.md Locked Decision 39); giving a guest a real, persisted plan is an identity and ownership question — who owns the rows before there is a user, and what happens at sign-up — and it is not a feature to bolt onto the cart path. None of the four is started, and none is a defect: each is a scoped absence with a named trigger for revisiting it. | `client/src/lib/earn-roles.ts` (EA); `shared/schema.ts` (`provider_services.maxConcurrentBookings`); CLAUDE.md Locked Decision 39 |
