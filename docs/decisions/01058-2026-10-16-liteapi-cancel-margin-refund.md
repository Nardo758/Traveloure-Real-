Nothing in `server/` reads this yet.) | **R487 — A LITEAPI CANCEL REFUND RETURNS OUR MARGIN IN THE SAME PROPORTION AS NUITÉE'S REFUND.** Decision-maker ruling (Leon), relayed in the Track A dispatch of Oct 16, 2026: "LiteAPI cancel refunds return our margin in the same proportion as Nuitée's refund." This settles the part of R471 (`2026-10-15-liteapi-cancel-with-charges`) that said "the refund maths is a pending founder call" as far as Traveloure's margin goes. Under LD 64 / LD 43 Nuitée is the merchant of record and Traveloure's earnings are the margin inside the rate, so the proportion is read from what R471 already stores: Nuitée's `refund_amount` against the total the traveler paid, both from `liteapi_bookings.cancel_amounts` and the booking row, in the same currency (a currency mismatch stays R471's `liteapi_refund_unverified` exception). A full refund returns all of our margin, a cancel with no refund returns none, and a partial refund returns that same fraction of it. No fee literal is introduced: the margin comes from the `hotel_margin_*` bands the booking was priced with (S1-d-2). **Not built here:** the computation, where the returned margin is recorded, and how it reaches Nuitée's payout reconciliation are the build lane's, which reports its design before writing code. | `docs/DECISIONS.md` | numeric citation R487; amends R471 `2026-10-15-liteapi-cancel-with-charges` (refund maths); LD 64; LD 43(c); merged 5b83b1c |
