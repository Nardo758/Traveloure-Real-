(e) `checkPlatformDraft` and `publishPost` refuse a post that carries Trending, Hidden Gem(s), geisha, back alley or illuminations, case-insensitive. Trending, Hidden Gem(s) and geisha reuse the existing patterns in `shared/banned-copy.ts`. Back alley and illuminations are blog-only and are not added to the item-title list or the site scan. A date is a calendar day. Today is not past. An event or trip window is past when its end day is before today. A series is past only when its latest instance has ended. Structured dates decide when the facts carry them. Prose dates decide only when they do not, and a Checked date line is ignored. Publish of a post tied to a city event uses that row's last day in the market zone and does not scan prose, so a fact stamp cannot refuse a future event. A post with no event uses prose dates in Asia/Tokyo. Refusals are `*_banned_copy` and `*_past_date` on the draft, and `banned_copy` and `past_date` (422) on publish. The post stays a draft. (f) A Fever coordinate is kept for matching and is not published. When the coordinate is missing and an address is present, the address is geocoded once with OpenStreetMap Nominatim (`PUBLIC_NOMINATIM_BASE_URL`), at most one request a second (`NOMINATIM_MIN_INTERVAL_MS`), User-Agent `Traveloure/1.0 (Kyoto event area match; +https://traveloure.com)`. The answer, including a miss, is stored in `fever_address_geocodes` (migration 401, primary key only, no DEFAULT, no CHECK, no extra index, declared in `shared/schema.ts`). A network failure is not stored. The point is placed with `placeServiceInNeighborhood` against Kyoto `city_neighborhoods` centre and radius. Outside every radius the area is unnamed. The public payload carries `areaName` and `osmCredit` and drops the street. The card shows the area name, and the sentence © OpenStreetMap contributors when the point came from Nominatim or an area was matched, because neighbourhood centres are OpenStreetMap data. A non-partner event still shows its venue.
