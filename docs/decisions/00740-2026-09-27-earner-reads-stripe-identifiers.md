Landed in #1122, `0a259edf4`.) | **R167 — EARNER READS EXPOSED STRIPE IDENTIFIERS INSIDE `booking_details` (defect found and fixed Sep 27, 2026; decision-maker asked for it to be recorded under its own name).** `sanitizeBookingForExpert` (`server/utils/data-sanitizer.ts`) is the projection every expert and provider booking read passes through. It stripped the Stripe COLUMNS (`stripePaymentIntentId`, `stripeDepositIntentId`, `stripeBalanceIntentId`, `idempotencyKey`) but never looked INSIDE `booking_details`, where the money path keeps its own records. On `main` three Stripe-bearing keys were already reaching experts and providers: the checkout's Stripe idempotency key (`stripeIdempotencyKey`), the §15c `reconciliationException` (which carries the PaymentIntent id) and the #1288 `outOfBandRefund` stamp (refund ids and the charge id); R162's `lateSuccessRefund` (a refund id) and R163's two new refund keys (`serviceBookingRefundAttempt`, with the refund's idempotency key, and `serviceBookingRefund`, with its refund id) would have joined them. Caught before any real booking existed. **Fix:** `EARNER_HIDDEN_BOOKING_DETAIL_KEYS` names eight keys (the six above plus `stripeAttemptAt` and `outOfBandRefundCleared`, which carries refund ids and the admin who cleared them) and strips them from `booking_details` for every non-`canSeeFull` role; the rest of `booking_details` is kept and an admin still sees the whole row. **Known limit, filed:** the list is a DENYLIST, so the next money key someone adds to `booking_details` leaks by default.
