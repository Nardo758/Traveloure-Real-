Docs only: no schema, no migration, no code.) | **R642 — MULTI-CURRENCY PRICING AND AN FX MARGIN (proposal; status PENDING).** Leon's ruling is needed. Nothing here is built. (1) Experts and providers set prices in their local currency (JPY for Kyoto). Payouts go out in that currency through Stripe Connect. (2) Travelers are charged in their own currency (USD at first) at a platform rate. The platform rate is the reference mid-market rate times (1 + fxMarginPct). fxMarginPct is admin-editable. The default is left blank for Leon to set; this row states no margin figure. (3) The rate is locked at checkout and stored on the booking: the reference rate, the source, the margin, and the time. Checkout shows the local price, the converted total, and a line saying 'includes a currency conversion margin of X%'. Refunds use the locked rate. (4) Platform fees stay percentages (provider 10%, expert 25%, traveler 7%). The $25 traveler-fee cap gets a per-currency value in admin. (5) Every amount is stored as an integer in the currency's smallest unit plus an ISO currency code. JPY has 0 decimals. (6) Margin revenue is the spread between the platform rate and Stripe's actual conversion cost. It is recorded separately in the ledger. (7) Open questions, left unanswered: the source of the reference rate; how often it refreshes; whether there is a margin on the payout leg; the consumer-disclosure check with counsel; any future wallet or transfer product goes through a licensed partner only.
