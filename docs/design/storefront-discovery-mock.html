Surfacing service-provider storefronts

A traveler discovers a provider through what they sell, and the storefront is the "everything from this seller" destination behind it. No standalone directory yet — that stays parked until a market has enough live storefronts to fill it honestly (§13, no empty aisles). This flow starts where the traveler starts: clicking Discover.

Parked: a browsable /providers directory. Stands up only when a market has ≥10 live storefronts, so it never renders as an empty aisle (standing ruling).
Audit findings (what's actually left to build). ① The Browse Services link is SHIPPED — the open call is placement: keep the shipped image-overlay affordance, or adopt this mock's explicit row. ② Partner/affiliate cards ("via Partners", curated content) must NEVER carry a storefront affordance — there is no storefront behind them (§13). ③ The real bottleneck is HANDLE ADOPTION: every link is gated on a claimed handle (nullable, no backfill), and the provider setup checklist has no "claim your handle" item — the highest-leverage build is that nudge. ④ Directory stays parked (≥10 live storefronts/market).
Why this shape. It matches how the platform already works: a seller is found through the thing they sell, and the storefront is the "more from them" destination. Shipping the Discover-services link now closes the last gap where a provider's own listings couldn't reach their storefront — the directory waits for supply density rather than launching empty.
