    Property builder — not yet specified

    This door stays locked until the rooms / nightly / date-range builder is designed (spec gap #1). Sending you into the single-service form would be worse than a locked door: that form has no rooms concept, no “per night” price unit, and no date-range availability, so it would collect the wrong answers and look finished.
