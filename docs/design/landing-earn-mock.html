Two fields filter the browse surface only. They never write trip state.
Destination actionPlan this destination
Uses the shared compact pulse CityCard and preserves encoded city navigation.
Frame decision. The coral hero action reads Plan my trip and remains the in-place AI-itinerary → comparison flow. The outline action reads Browse local experts and resolves to `/experts?role=local_expert`. The earn cards display the roles their unchanged `?track=` aliases resolve to.
Data boundary. City cards, stats, and testimonials are shown as data-shaped states. The compact city examples are visual content for review, not claims about current database rows; production continues to source them from the existing APIs.
Hard stop at the frame. This is a static design artifact. Do not build the landing component, modify `TrendingCities`/`CityCard`, change handlers or test IDs, add photos or inventory, or update tests until this frame and `LANDING_SPEC.md` are reviewed and the next gate is authorized.
