How the preview stays honest

Every figure is derived server-side from the plan itself, and any claim without a real baseline is left off rather than guessed (CLAUDE.md §13). What you see on each proposal is exactly what the data supports — no filler zeros.

Money saved

Baseline total vs. proposal total, both from the optimizer. Shown only when there's a real positive baseline to beat.

Costs more, said plainly

A pricier proposal never hides it — Fewer Hops shows “Costs $40 more” beside its time saving.

Shorter drive time

Sum of located transport-leg minutes, both plans. Time only — distance is never a headline claim (§21 L3).

Omitted when unknown

Riverside Focus shows no travel chip — a stop wasn’t located, so no drive-time delta is claimed. The current plan shows no chips at all.

Trending & in season

Pulled from live destination signals for Kyoto and your travel dates; each line disappears when there's nothing real to show.
