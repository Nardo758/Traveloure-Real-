v2 vs v1
What changed. Hero: image card → live mini-bento + ticker; static placeholder → typed real requests. Sections: card borders removed on entries, experiences, cities, steps, numbers, earn; hairlines and photos carry the structure. Coral count: 4 (hero, occasions, earn, final) — one per section. Components unchanged — the bento tiles are the feed's compact tiles at a smaller size; CityCard gets a chrome="none" prop; experiences use the family card's photo + title only.
Data the hero depends on. Top trending city must have an anchor-eligible expert, ≥1 gem, ≥1 bookable service to look like this; otherwise the grid collapses honestly. Kyoto after the seed lane qualifies; production will show whatever real rows exist. That is the point.
