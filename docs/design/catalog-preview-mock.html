The mapping toolkit — what a provider could show travelers

Everything below is folded into the map above except the last column, which is still your pick. Each stays honest — no invented drive-times, no guessed pins, real data only (§13).

Traveler layers · on map

  • Start / meeting pin — placed & confirmed by you, never geocoded by guess.
  • Numbered route stops — dashed = sequence, never fake driving directions.
  • Coverage radius or a pickup route — pick one, the other isn’t erased.
  • Pick-up / drop-off + honest “2 of 3 located”, unpinned kept in the rail.

Business layers · on map

  • Demand heat — where real requests cluster, so you see where the money is.
  • Coverage-gap alert — demand outside your radius (“18 near Arashiyama — extend?”).
  • Travel-surcharge zone — your fee beyond a distance ring; listing config, not a platform fee.
  • Market reach — travelers inside your radius, to size a market before expanding.

Also proposed · your pick

  • Photo on the pin — a snapshot of the meetup spot (“the red torii gate”).
  • Nearest transit marker · time-at-stop (your declared dwell).
  • Approximate-area vs exact pin — soft zone for privacy, precise on booking.
  • Saved route templates · availability by area × day · map thumbnail on the card · “Route” share image.

Locked, and still open

  • One door — decided. Add New Service now opens Workstation, where you pick service / bundle / property. One creation flow, lighter code, one thing for providers to learn.
  • Book + price on every card, with a per-listing “Card shows” control in Manage — price on/off and Booking = Instant / Request / Hidden. (Flip to Manage, change a card, flip back to Preview to see it.) Rides your existing Instant Booking setting.
  • Open for you: which new mapping ideas above do you want in v1? · keep the hover Edit on Preview cards or strip it? · allow “Hidden price” on all services or only quote-based ones?
