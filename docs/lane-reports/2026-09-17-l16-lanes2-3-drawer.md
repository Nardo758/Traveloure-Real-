**Three decisions inside those five, because they are decisions and not mechanics.**

### (i) The drawer does NOT read `GET /api/pricing`

Brief §5.4 points the apply control's price at the pricing bundle's `aiTaskCents`. **D-48 — the
later ruling — extended the proposals GET with a server-resolved `aiTask` block precisely so there
is "no second entitlement rail, no second fee read (§18 rule 1)".** Both are the same
`concierge:ai_task` band, but two reads of one band are two numbers free to disagree in flight, and
one of them is behind the gate this surface already runs and the other is public. The drawer takes
the proposals GET's. **§8 is satisfied either way: no literal exists in the client**, and A1/A2 pin
both facts. The brief's §7.2 row carries the correction.

### (ii) The advisor arm waits for the SERVER; the owner arm does not

`tripRole === "expert"` comes from `getTripRole`, whose advisor branch is `isTripAdvisor` — which
grants **`pending`**, correctly, for READING the plan. It therefore cannot tell a §12 WRITE advisor
from a pending one, and **restating §12's status list on the client would be a second copy of a rule
whose home is `server/utils/trip-advisor.ts`**. So the advisor arm asks the server: the proposal
log's own gate IS `requireWriteAccess`, so a successful read is the proof and a 401/403 is the
refusal. A still-loading read and a failed read also draw nothing — **an omitted control is honest,
one that appears and then vanishes is not** (§13). The OWNER passes both tiers by construction.

### (iii) The staleness window is not computed here at all

D-50 (c)'s window is SERVER config. A client that re-derived it would be a second authority that
disagrees the day the knob moves. Expiry reaches this surface **only as the server's own refusal**,
and the RE-ASK offer is decided by the server's **reason code**
(`stale_catalog_price` · `listing_unavailable` · `protected_item` · `refunded`) and never by a
message match — never a retry of the same proposal, never a silent reprice.
