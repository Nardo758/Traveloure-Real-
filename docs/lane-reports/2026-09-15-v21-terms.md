## 2. The one wording judgement, and why it went the way it did

The brief's example sentence ends "…and is shown to you **before you pay**". **That sentence was
deliberately NOT used**, because all four literals are about **EARNER earnings**, and the traveler-facing
half of it would have been a new §13 lie replacing the old one: ledger `2026-09-08-cart-fee-line`
(`client/src/pages/cart.tsx:1188-1199`) rules that the provider's commission is a **deduction from the
payout**, "neither a line on the buyer's summary nor a term of their total" — the traveler is never shown
a platform-fee line before paying. So each replacement takes the brief's other branch, quoted verbatim in
the dispatch: *"Where the sentence is about EXPERT/PROVIDER earnings, say the share is set by the fee
schedule and shown in the earner console, not a range."* The earner console shows net earnings and payout
records (`client/src/pages/provider/earnings.tsx`, `/expert/money` · `/provider/money`), so
"shown in your Traveloure console on each earning and payout record" is a fact, not a promise. An earlier
draft said "before you accept a booking"; that was cut because it is not verifiable from the code.

### T1's shape, stated because it diverges from the brief's literal wording

The dispatch asked for "no `\d+\s*-\s*\d+\s*%` **or** `\d+%` range". A blanket "no `\d+%` anywhere in
terms.tsx" is **not implementable without changing legal content this lane is forbidden to touch**, because
three real numbers remain and all three are correct:

- **`2.9% + $0.30 per transaction (Stripe fees)`** — a **third-party processor's own published
  pass-through rate**, not a Traveloure commission or margin. The punchlist row named four literals and
  this was not one of them.
- **`50% refund at least 48 hours before`** / **`50% refund if you cancel at least 7 days`** — the
  cancellation policy.
- **`you receive 100% of what you paid`** — the full-refund promise the refund rail performs.

A refund percentage is a share of money the traveler **already paid**, under a policy shown to them before
they confirm (`terms.tsx:333`); it is the opposite of a fee rate, and §8 governs fee/commission/margin
literals. So T1 pins *no range* + *no commission/share as a percentage*, and **T7 asserts all three
survivors**, so the sweep can never be satisfied by quietly deleting legal content. This is recorded in the
test header, the ledger row and here rather than left as an undocumented narrowing.

## 4. The fee gate was NOT changed — §18d

`client` **is** in `scripts/phase2-fee-gate.sh`'s `ROOTS`, so V-21 was **not a root gap**. Pass A/C match a
FIXED value list these numbers are not on; Pass B needs the number on the **same line** as a fee-ish
identifier followed by `:` or `=`, which prose never is. All three predicates returned zero on this file
while it published four rates — that is the gate's **stated negative space**, and §18d says to read green as
*green-within-stated-bounds*, not to widen the predicate. Widening a fee grep to read prose would flag every
refund percentage and every legal sentence in the repo. **The gate is untouched; the committed pin is the
layer.** The gate still passes, and still prints its one filed `fee-literal-debt:#PS2`.
