What this ratifies

Build-first, distribute-later

One "New build" entry; the client/store/social choice moves into the Distribute panel. Server change: a listing is created FROM an existing build ("ship to store"), instead of listing-then-trip.

the v2 model
Templates lane sunsets — seller side first

Retire the seller surface and block new listings now; consumer wind-down is its own decision-maker-read phase. Buyers keep purchased itineraries.

gated on prod counts
Content Studio → Distribute → Social

The studio page becomes a redirect; share card, caption AI, and Instagram publish live on the build they promote.
