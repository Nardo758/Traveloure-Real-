Design spec · awaiting ratification

Chat, rebuilt around the conversation

The messaging page currently asks one screen to be two things: an inbox and an expert directory. This spec separates them, gives the thread a real home at every width, and brings plan activity into the conversation where it belongs.

The directory buries the inbox

Every expert on the platform renders inline beneath your conversations. Below ~1024px the panes stack, so the thread you opened sits far under the fold.

No master–detail on small screens

List and thread both try to exist at once instead of one replacing the other, which is what every messaging app does for a reason.

Plan activity is invisible here

Delivery, approval and suggestions all fire real notifications — but they surface one tab away, so the conversation goes silent at the exact moment it matters.

Desktop — two panes, two scrolls

Conversations only on the left. Finding an expert is a deliberate action, not ambient page content.

“New” stays “New.” An expert with no reviews shows no invented score — the honest-ratings rule already live on the cards carries into the sheet unchanged.

Plan activity, inline

Read-only markers in chronological position, sourced from the notifications that already fire. They are a signal, not a second copy of the record.

One home for the data. These render from existing notification rows; marking-as-read stays owned by Inbox and the bell. Nothing is copied into the message table.

2

No guessing. An event renders only when its trip resolves to this conversation's partner through the real advisor link — otherwise it does not appear here at all.

3

No dead links. If a target can't be resolved, the row renders without the action rather than pointing somewhere broken.
