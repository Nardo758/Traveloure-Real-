12 entries (from 15) — same structure as the provider view. Three selling catalogs → My Offerings; two fulfillment entries → Orders; DMO Library and Content Studio's creation half fold into the Workspace (where their consumers already live: the DMO picker is wired into the trip builder, and the Studio's content library had no backend to relocate). Role-variant labels ride the same mechanism they use today.
The make/distribute split (design principle): creation lives in the Workspace; distribution lives in Share & Promote — because event-driven prompts ("new 5★ review — share it") fire from dashboard state, not from inside an authoring session. The no-trip Workspace launchpad keeps standalone DMO research reachable without an active trip.

Why this shape is cheap

The expert sidebar already does the hard part — buildMenuGroups(expertType) is a role-parameterized nav config. The unified sidebar extends that one function to cover the provider role and deletes the duplicate provider component; both files currently repeat identical chrome (logo tile, group labels, active state, footer) with the same inline hex values. One config, one component, two role renderings — and the merge is the natural moment to tokenize the inline coral/gradient values the mapping pass flagged as duplicated.

The Workspace-as-hub tweak (ratified direction, this iteration): both role navs now share one structure — Dashboard · Orders · Calendar · Workspace · Messages / My Offerings · Share & Promote · Reviews · Earnings · Analytics. The Workspace is the make surface (builder + ingredients: DMO Library, creation tools; provider mode wraps the canonical ServiceForm per §5); Share & Promote is the distribute surface (event-driven prompts, captions, links, the Instagram rail pending the Tier-2 decision). The folds land on existing seams: the DMO picker is already wired into the trip builder, the workspace shell is already server-resolved dual-mode (a provider mode is a third case, not a new system), and Content Studio's unpersisted content library means the fold moves UI, not backend.
