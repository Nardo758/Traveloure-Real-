Distribution formats: where the plan is going picks its shape

v1 of this mockup read "destination" as geography — corrected. A build ships to four distribution destinations (§17: Client · Store · Social · Direct), and the channel picks the structure. Experience type flavors it; market (Kyoto) styles it. A plan going to Social media gets a social-media-driven format, not the Trip Card.

1 · How a render picks its format

No new data is stored. The channel is intrinsic to the surface doing the rendering; type and market come from fields every build already has. Fallback chain per channel — every existing surface keeps rendering exactly as today until a more specific format matches.

Decisions this mockup encodes

Corrected axis, re-ratified.

"Destination" = the distribution destination. Format = channel × experience type × market ("option 1 & 3"). The channel picks the structure; type flavors; market styles.

Social produces both.

The story-style visual rendering AND the caption + share-image pack — the latter extends the existing promo-text service and share-image layout, never a parallel engine.

The v1 Kyoto work survives inside Client/Store.

kyoto-cultural neighborhood grouping and the wedding venue-timeline become client:*/store:* market×type entries — nothing thrown away.

Derived, not stored.

Channel is intrinsic to the rendering surface; type + market come from trips.eventType/destination. No migration, no publish-push trap.

Honest slots (§13).

Story frames, seasonal notes, maps, and counts render only from real build data; nothing is invented to fill a frame.

Build order.

F1 registry + extract client:default (behavior-identical refactor) → F2 client Kyoto/event structures → F3 social kit (story view + caption pack in the Distribute panel) → F4 store layouts + direct link-preview polish.
