# Walkthrough — Trip Pass traveler — 2026-08-31

**Persona:** Trip Pass traveler (`persona-kyoto-trip-pass@traveloure.test`, role `user`, one seeded
manual-provenance pass on trip `32db2994…`)
**Goal:** "I bought a Trip Pass — does my covered trip actually waive the fee, and does the app stop
me buying a second one?"
**Env:** local dev server (`http://localhost:5000`), seeded personas, Stripe test mode. Never production.
**Harness:** ruling `2026-08-30-autonomous-walkthroughs`; matrix row 3 (next after the row-2 traveler
report of 2026-08-30).

## Path actually taken

`/my-trips` signed out (silent redirect to `/`) → sign-in modal → `/dashboard` → `/my-trips` → the
covered trip's slip `/plans/32db2994…` (`trip-pass-card-active`) → `/services/b586f80a…` (Zen
Mindfulness Retreat, $280) → Add to Cart → `/cart` → **Proceed to Payment → Complete Booking with
`POST /api/checkout` intercepted and aborted in-browser** (the request never reached the server; no
claim, no PaymentIntent, no charge) → `GET /api/cart/fee-preview` with and without the covered
`tripId` → `/pricing` → "Get a Trip Pass" → `/dashboard` pick-a-trip toast → `Create New` → a second,
**uncovered** Kyoto trip (`310c2e96…`, TRV-202608-00003) → its slip's `trip-pass-card-offer`
(**I did not click "Get the Trip Pass"** — THE LINE).

**Answer to the persona's question, plainly:** the covered trip *says* the fee is waived, and the
checkout total is **identical to the cent with and without the pass**. The pass cannot be bought
twice from the UI (the covered trip renders the active card and no buy control), so the second half
of the goal is satisfied.

## Findings

### F-TP1 — the $19 Trip Pass waives a fee the traveler is never charged; the total does not move
- **CLASS:** BUG (money path — escalated, not fixed)
- **SCREENSHOT:** `docs/testing/walkthroughs/2026-08-31-trip-pass/shot-06-trip-view.png` (slip claim),
  `shot-09-cart.png` (the charge), `shot-13-fee-preview-json.png` (the server's own numbers)
- **EXPECTATION VIOLATED:** CLAUDE.md §13 (render what the data actually says) and ruling
  `2026-08-29-trip-pass`, which ratifies the waiver as carrying an **"honest counterfactual"**
  (`billedOnDirectPathToday:false` — "resolver-only today, not suppressed real billing"). The
  honesty was built into the server field and then **dropped at the render**.
- **EVIDENCE:** same cart, two reads of the same endpoint —
  - `GET /api/cart/fee-preview?tripId=32db2994…` → `platformFeeTotal 33.6, total 313.6,
    tripPassFeeWaiver:{waived:true, wouldHaveBeenAmountTotal:19.6, billedOnDirectPathToday:false}`
  - `GET /api/cart/fee-preview` (no tripId) → `platformFeeTotal 33.6, total 313.6,
    tripPassFeeWaiver:null`
  Identical money. Meanwhile the slip states, unqualified, "**service fee waived on this trip**"
  (`TripPassCard.tsx:100`) and the sales card states "$19 · … · **service fee waived**"
  (`shot-19-new-trip-slip.png`), and `itinerary.tsx:742` would paint a teal "**Included in your Trip
  Pass — service fee waived**" banner above a total reduced by $0.00.
- **FILE:LINE HYPOTHESIS:** `client/src/components/plancard/TripPassCard.tsx:100` and `:~120`;
  `client/src/pages/itinerary.tsx:738-747`; the `billedOnDirectPathToday` flag resolved at
  `server/routes/payments.routes.ts:~2005` is never surfaced to any of them.
- **SEVERITY:** high — this is the paid product's headline benefit, and the traveler pays $19 for a
  line that changes nothing they are billed.
- **DISPOSITION:** escalate to Leon. Money path, and what the copy *should* say (qualify the claim, or
  make the waiver bite) is a product ruling, not a patch I may invent.

### F-TP2 — the traveler is charged a 12% uncapped "Platform fee", not the published 7% / cap-$25 service fee
- **CLASS:** BUG (money path — escalated, not fixed)
- **SCREENSHOT:** `shot-09-cart.png` (Subtotal $280.00 · Platform fee **$33.60** · Total $313.60),
  `shot-07-service-detail.png` (the contradicting provider-side sentence)
- **EXPECTATION VIOLATED:** `docs/design/PRICING_AND_FEATURE_MAP.md` L73 ("Traveler service fee |
  traveler | **7% of booking, cap $25**") and L147 ("**The free tier's price is the 7% service fee.**
  The Trip Pass waives it on that trip's bookings; that line leads the offer") — the citation of
  record per the pricing-surfaces audit brief. `GET /api/pricing` publishes `serviceFeePct:7`,
  `serviceFeeCapCents:2500`, and the landing "How it works" renders exactly that
  (`client/src/components/landing/how-it-works.tsx:81`).
- **EVIDENCE:** the amount actually added to the traveler's total is
  `itemPrice * (1 - itemExpertShare) + insurance` — the **provider commission** (`proRateStandard:12`
  from the same pricing bundle) = 12% of $280 = **$33.60**, uncapped. The published traveler fee on
  the same booking would be $19.60 (7%, under the $25 cap) — which is exactly the number the waiver
  records as `wouldHaveBeenAmountTotal`. So the traveler is quoted one fee model and charged another.
  The service page compounds it: "A platform service fee is **deducted from each booking; the
  provider receives the remainder**" — but the cart **adds** it to the traveler's total.
- **FILE:LINE HYPOTHESIS:** `server/routes/payments.routes.ts:1987` (`previewPlatformFeeTotal +=
  itemPrice * (1 - itemExpertShare) + itemInsuranceFee`) and its twin in the checkout charge loop;
  labeled "Platform fee" at `client/src/pages/cart.tsx` order summary.
- **SEVERITY:** high — a published price the checkout does not honor.
- **DISPOSITION:** escalate to Leon. Whether the 12% add-on is the intended model or the 7%/cap is,
  is a pricing ruling; either way one of the two surfaces is lying to the traveler today.

### F-TP3 — the cart never sends `tripId`, so no real booking can ever carry the Trip Pass waiver
- **CLASS:** BUG (money path — escalated, not fixed)
- **SCREENSHOT:** `shot-11-checkout-body-captured.png` (the confirm step; the request was aborted
  in-browser and the page's own honest "You have not been charged" state is visible)
- **EXPECTATION VIOLATED:** ruling `2026-08-29-persona-coverage-complete` asserts "the traveler-fee
  waiver **recorded on a real checkout booking row** (`booking_details.tripPassFeeWaiver`)". From the
  product that row can never be written.
- **EVIDENCE:** the intercepted body of the one and only client call to `POST /api/checkout` is
  `{"currency":"USD","idempotencyKey":"bcd24329-…","useSavedCard":false}` — **no `tripId`**. Server
  side the waiver pre-pass is gated `if (tripId && await coversAction(...))`
  (`server/routes/payments.routes.ts:1269`), so it never runs. `GET /api/trip-context` returns `{}`
  and the cart's trip strip reads "General / Add your travel dates" — there is no UI anywhere that
  binds a cart to the covered trip. DB confirms: `SELECT count(*) FROM service_bookings WHERE
  booking_details ? 'tripPassFeeWaiver'` → **0**. The persona spec passes `tripId` at the API level,
  which is why the automated journey is green and the product is not.
- **FILE:LINE HYPOTHESIS:** `client/src/pages/cart.tsx:760-770` (checkout body omits `tripId`).
- **SEVERITY:** medium (today it changes no money because of F-TP1; it is the wiring that makes
  F-TP1 unfixable-in-place).
- **DISPOSITION:** escalate to Leon.

### F-TP4 — `POST /api/checkout` reads `tripId` from `req.body` straight into the entitlement gate, unverified
- **CLASS:** BUG (money path — escalated, not fixed)
- **SCREENSHOT:** `shot-11-checkout-body-captured.png` (the body the client controls),
  `shot-13-fee-preview-json.png` (the sibling endpoint that *does* check)
- **EXPECTATION VIOLATED:** CLAUDE.md §14 (a `req.body` field must never reach a money decision),
  extended by §18 (the *selector* that decides a fee is as privileged as the amount) and §19
  (privileged-field mass-assignment). Ruling `2026-08-29-trip-pass` scopes the pass to **one trip**.
- **FAILURE SCENARIO:** `const { tripId, notes, idempotencyKey } = req.body`
  (`server/routes/payments.routes.ts:756`) flows unchecked to
  `if (tripId && (await coversAction(String(tripId), "traveler_service_fee")))` (`:1269`).
  `coversAction` is per-trip by design and checks no owner. Nothing between those two lines verifies
  the trip belongs to the session user. So a crafted request naming **any** trip that holds an active
  pass — including one bought by someone else — takes the waiver, and a traveler who buys one $19
  pass can name that tripId on every future checkout, defeating the per-trip scope. The sibling
  `GET /api/cart/fee-preview` ownership-checks the same parameter on the documented
  `resolveTripIdParam` pattern (`:1830-1850`); the charge path does not.
- **WHY THE GUARD MISSED IT:** `scripts/check-money-endpoints.cjs`'s `FIELD_RE` covers
  `amount|price|userId|rate|share|commission|split`. `tripId` is an **authorization grant**, the blind
  spot §19c names in writing ("an amount, a `status`, an authorization grant (`#PS16`) is still
  invisible to it"). Green here is green-within-stated-bounds (§18d).
- **SEVERITY:** medium-high — inert today only because F-TP1 means the waiver moves no money; it
  becomes a live hole the moment `billedOnDirectPathToday` turns true.
- **DISPOSITION:** escalate to Leon. I did **not** open a fix PR: money-path spine, and the
  guardrail's `[guarded]` allowance is not a licence to edit the checkout claim path unattended.
  (**I did not exploit it either** — the claim is a code read plus the aborted-request capture; no
  cross-account request was ever sent.)

## What was correct (worth pinning)

- **`trip-pass-card-active` renders exactly as ruled** — teal check tile, mono sub-line, and no buy
  control anywhere on the covered trip, so a second purchase is unreachable from the UI
  (`shot-06`). The 409 rail is never even needed by a real user.
- **The offer card obeys the grammar** (`shot-19`): teal `Get the Trip Pass`, price `$19` in mono
  read from the `plans` row via `GET /api/trips/:id/trip-pass`, coral left to Finalize.
- **Absent data renders as absent** — trip card stats read `6 DAYS / 0 ACTIVITIES / 0 TRANSIT LEGS /
  **-** TRANSIT TIME`; the service page's availability says "No availability published yet for this
  month" rather than inventing slots (§13).
- **The failed checkout tells the truth**: "Your payment could not be processed. **You have not been
  charged** — please try again."
- **ODbL attribution present** on the service map (`shot-07`), per §22.

## THE LINE — honored

Read-only against money. `POST /api/checkout` was intercepted and **aborted in the browser** at the
confirm step, so it never reached the server: no claim row, no PaymentIntent, no charge. I did not
click "Get the Trip Pass" on the uncovered trip. F-TP4 was established by reading the code, not by
sending a cross-account request. Not production.

**Data this walk created in dev, deliberately and on-goal:** one cart item (Zen Mindfulness Retreat)
left in the persona's cart, and one uncovered trip `310c2e96-310c-4c7b-bbc2-ce733a748e29`
("Kyoto Trip", TRV-202608-00003, Nov 10–14 2026) created to reach the offer-card branch. Both are
ordinary persona actions; recorded here so a later walk knows where they came from.

## Nothing was fixed on this walk

All four BUG-class findings sit on the money path (`server/routes/payments.routes.ts`,
`client/src/pages/cart.tsx`, the Trip Pass copy that sells a benefit). Per the harness guardrails a
money-path finding escalates rather than self-fixes, and F-TP1/F-TP2 additionally turn on a pricing
decision that is Leon's to make. The four JUDGMENT findings are filed, not patched.

## Follow-up for the harness itself

`journey-traveler.spec.ts`'s covered branch is green because it calls the API with `tripId` in hand.
The product never sends one (F-TP3). A regression test that binds the waiver assertion to **the body
`cart.tsx` actually posts** — not to a hand-built request — would have caught this on the day it
landed, and is the deterministic guard this walk's catch should become.
