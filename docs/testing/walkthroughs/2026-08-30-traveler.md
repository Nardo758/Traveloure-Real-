## Findings

### F-T1 — `/experiences` auto-opens the "New plan" chooser over its own browse grid
- **CLASS:** JUDGMENT
- **SCREENSHOT:** `2026-08-30-traveler/shot-04-experiences-modal.png`
- **WHAT HAPPENS:** Navigating to `/experiences` (a Marketplace browse surface titled "Plan
  Your Perfect Experience", with a grid of experience-type cards — Travel, Birthday, Boys
  Trip, Girls Trip, Date Night, Corporate Events…) immediately pops the global **"New plan"**
  chooser modal (Destination prefilled "Kyoto, Japan", Start/End date, Travelers) on top of
  that grid. The modal is a full overlay: a click aimed at an experience card behind it lands
  on the overlay, so a traveler who came to *browse* must dismiss the modal before the page is
  usable. On this walk it blocked the add-to-cart path entirely.
- **EXPECTATION VIOLATED:** No ratified ruling I could load says the planning chooser must
  auto-open on the `/experiences` route. The planning-entry work (the global chooser) is meant
  to open on a *planning entry action*; auto-opening on page load conflicts with the page's own
  browse purpose. The skill's behavior rubric — "a planning entry opens the slip not a details
  card" — governs entry *clicks*, not a route load. Because no ruling settles whether this route
  should auto-open the chooser, this is a JUDGMENT call, not a BUG.
- **FILE:LINE HYPOTHESIS:** the `/experiences` page component (`client/src/pages/experiences*.tsx`)
  mounting/opening the global planning-chooser on mount rather than on an entry action.
- **SEVERITY:** medium (blocks the primary browse→book path for a traveler who arrives to browse).
- **DISPOSITION:** **Escalate to Leon.** JUDGMENT class → the harness never auto-fixes it.
  Question for the decision-maker: on `/experiences`, should the chooser auto-open, or should
  the grid be browsable with the chooser one click away (as on the landing "Where do you want
  to begin?" tiles)?
