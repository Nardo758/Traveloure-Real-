---
name: Billboard market inventory
description: Why the landing billboard's live-market selection is limited by curated fallback coverage
---

Select a live billboard market only when it has both a byline-gated slot-one listing and truthful, credited curated fallback inventory for all three slots.

**Why:** The existing curated copy and photos describe Kyoto. If another market were selected without its own approved fallback inventory, an absent gem or bookable listing would show Kyoto cards next to a different city's live expert, or invite a misleading photo label. A live listing in another market alone is not enough.

**How to apply:** Before enabling another market, provide and credit its per-slot fallback cards, then test mixed live/curated states and the no-slot-one case. Do not reuse Kyoto photos or copy as if they represented another city.
