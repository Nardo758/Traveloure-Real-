---
name: Marketplace surface scope
description: Visual scope decision for Traveloure's public Marketplace routes.
---

Use the full editorial Field Guide treatment on the commerce-focused Ready-Made Trips and Services surfaces. Keep Destinations and Events anchored in their established city-grid and calendar experiences, with only restrained shared navigation, typography, spacing, and color adjustments.

**Why:** The commerce surfaces benefit from a stronger browse-and-compare visual system. Destinations and Events carry denser travel-intelligence workflows whose existing hierarchy should remain familiar and easy to scan.

**How to apply:** When extending Marketplace styling, scope substantial card, filter, and editorial layout changes to the commerce surfaces. Do not replace the destination cards or global calendar just to make the four routes visually identical.
