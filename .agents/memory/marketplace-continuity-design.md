---
name: Marketplace continuity design
description: Durable visual-direction rule for Marketplace detail, storefront, and Ready-Made pages.
---

Marketplace post-click pages should retain the live Traveloure language: practical cool-light surfaces, the existing primary pink conversion cue, concise UI typography, and the page-specific commerce models already in production. Use the Field Guide work to improve information hierarchy, fact grouping, and action clarity—not to replace this language with its warm editorial palette and typography.

**Why:** The current Provider Storefront already has the strongest structural relationship to the Marketplace direction, while Service Detail and Ready-Made each carry different essential purchase behavior. A literal reskin would make those related pages feel like a separate product and risk obscuring their distinct trust models.

**How to apply:** Preserve the Service Detail booking rail and availability flow, keep the Provider Storefront services-first with compact trust evidence, and retain Ready-Made's protected itinerary preview and editable-after-purchase promise. Apply shared hierarchy improvements only where they do not blur those jobs.
